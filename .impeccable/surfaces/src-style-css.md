## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a printed-tin wind-up toy city; refuses the neon-on-dark and flat-tile puzzle app.
OWN-WORLD: Sea-teal lithographed ground with a printed motif, tomato tin tray with an inked rim line, ink-blue road bed. Flat hard-edged litho vehicles with printed windows, wheels and seam tabs; black-and-white checker cab as hero. Bowlby One lettering, Barlow Semi Condensed labels. No glow.
STORY: The player sees a toy, picks it up, reads the jam at a glance, works it out, and earns a stamped tin star.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Huge stacked GRID LOCK lettering, a printed road strip with tin vehicles parading beneath it, a tomato PLAY plate, levels and save ID below.
FORM: Tin toy city, position 1 of 7 (user chose the pick card); seed key 3f6376fb.
Signature: vehicles lift when grabbed and clack down when released; stars stamp in on a clear.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
