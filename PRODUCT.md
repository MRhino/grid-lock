# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
One player: the owner, on an iPhone home-screen install, in spare moments, working through hard puzzles.

## Product Purpose
Grid Lock is a sliding-traffic puzzle. Slide cars and trucks along their lanes to get one target car out through the gate. It should be a satisfying brain-teaser: focused thinking, and a real feeling of achievement when a jam clears.

## Positioning
A personal, ad-free puzzle with 300 generated levels, each with an exact computed par.

## Operating Context
Portrait phone, one-handed or two-thumbed dragging, short sessions resumed mid-level, offline-capable PWA.

## Capabilities and Constraints
6x6 board, cars (2 long) and trucks (3 long), one exit on the third row. Undo, restart, hint, moves vs par, 1-3 stars, tiers (Green Light, Commuter, Roadworks, Standstill, Total Gridlock) plus Warm-Up, resume, cloud save ID, version string and update check on the start screen. The player gave free rein on all visual identity, including piece colours and whether pieces read as vehicles.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Grid Lock. Inspired by a commercial childhood puzzle whose name, card layouts and look must stay out of the game.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, press or player data. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Thinking comes first: the board state must be readable at a glance.
- Every piece's axis of movement must be obvious.
- Clearing a jam should feel earned.
- Progress is never lost.
